\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

In this experiment we recorded our own single-channel Fp1 EEG with a BrainLink headset (three members, 30 recordings each) and built a pipeline to classify the Relax, Focus and Stroop states under Leave-One-Subject-Out cross-validation. Each 8~s segment (75\% overlap) was denoised with DC removal, a 60~Hz notch and a 1--45~Hz band-pass, and described by 32 features: the distribution of 1~s window features (relative theta/alpha/beta power, $\theta/\beta$ and $\alpha/\beta$ ratios, amplitude and kurtosis), followed by a subject-wise z-score. Four classifiers were compared on exactly the same features. Shrinkage LDA performed best with a mean LOSO accuracy of 82.5\%, followed by the SVM (80.3\%), the MLP (79.4\%) and the Random Forest (76.0\%). The ablation study shows that the subject-wise normalization contributed the most (+15.7 points), followed by the time-domain features, the window band-power features and filtering. The goal of $\geq 75\%$ accuracy was met by all four classifiers, and the two best models (LDA and SVM) also exceeded 80\%. The accuracy differs strongly between members (0.705 to 0.952 for the best model), and the results are based on only three subjects, so they should be regarded as optimistic estimates rather than a general subject-independent performance.
